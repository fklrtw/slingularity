# Slingularity Terminology

To maintain consistency across the codebase, documentation, and communications, the following terms MUST be used:

*   **Spaceship:** The entity the player controls. (Do not use "ship" or "spacecraft").
*   **Annihilate:** The action of removing or destroying a Planet or the Spaceship. (Do not use "destroy", "disintegrate", or "death").
*   **Atmosphere:** The zone around a planet where its gravity affects the Spaceship, visually signified by a faint white glow. Drag increases deeper within this zone. (Do not use "gravitational zone", "sphere of influence", or "gravity well").
*   **Comet:** A fast-moving, high-eccentricity celestial body that serves as a high-reward skip target.
*   **Comet Tail:** The dynamic, physics-driven trail of particles left behind by a Comet.
*   **Luminance Rings:** Multi-layered planar glow layers centered on the sun that scale with its radius. (Do not use "glow spheres" or "halo").
